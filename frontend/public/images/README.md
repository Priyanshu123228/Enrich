# Enrich Beauty Parlour — Local Media Assets

Place your genuine salon photos and videos into the designated subfolders below:

## 📁 Subfolder Directory Guide:

* `salon/`: Studio interior, styling stations, waiting lounge, and building exterior photos.
* `bridal/`: Real client bridal makeup, pre-wedding HD looks, and jewelry/hair styling photos.
* `services/`: Hydrafacial treatment, hair keratin, coloring, and skincare photos.
* `staff/`: Portrait photos of certified beauticians and hair stylists.
* `videos/`: Short salon tour clips, transformation reels, and client testimonial videos (.mp4).

---

## 📐 Recommended Image & Video Specifications:

* **Image Formats**: `.webp`, `.jpg`, `.png`
* **Recommended Dimensions**:
  * Banners & Studio Photos: `1200 x 800 px`
  * Service & Cosmetic Products: `800 x 800 px` (Square 1:1)
  * Staff Portraits: `600 x 600 px`
* **Video Format**: `.mp4` (H.264 encoded, under 15 MB for optimal loading speed)

---

## 💻 How to Reference in Code:
Any file placed here is instantly accessible via:
```jsx
<img src="/images/salon/your-photo.jpg" alt="Enrich Salon Sikar" />
```
